The ABCDE Rule for Mole Monitoring

A — Asymmetry
One half of a mole doesn't match the other half.
B — Border
Irregular, ragged, or blurry borders instead of smooth edges.
C — Color
Multiple shades of brown, black, red, or white in one spot.
D — Diameter
Larger than 6mm (about the size of a pencil eraser).
E — Evolving
Any mole that is changing in size, shape, color, or bleeding.
